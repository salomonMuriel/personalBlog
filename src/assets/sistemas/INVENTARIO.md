## Guía Colegio CRM (sales CRM for a school directory)

Source: the CRM's local design previews (`/preview/a/*`) running on the existing dev server, fixture data only. The fixtures hold real staff names and lightly altered real school names, so every one was swapped in the DOM before capture: staff became Valentina Rojas, Juliana Mora, Daniela and Andrés Silva; school contacts became Sara Molina, Jorge Ruiz, Natalia Vega and others; all 36 schools got invented names; avatar and school initials were recomputed from the fake names. A text check of the rendered page found none of the original names, and an OCR pass over the final files confirmed it.

- `guiacolegio-hoy-1440.webp`: Guía Colegio CRM. The salesperson's day: overdue and today's tasks (several created by automations), unanswered replies, agenda and deals in closing.
- `guiacolegio-tablero-1440.webp`: Guía Colegio CRM. Sales pipeline board by stage, with deal value, days in stage, heat score, next task and the latest buying signal.
- `guiacolegio-analitica-1440.webp`: Guía Colegio CRM. Sales analytics: won value against target, weighted pipeline, funnel by stage, wins per month and close rate by source. Check: figures are fixture values from `src/design/fixtures/analytics.ts`, not company results; no names on screen except the fake signed-in user.

Source: a copy of the repo run locally against a throwaway Postgres in Docker, built from the repo's migrations and filled by an invented seed: 28 people with diagnósticos generated by the site's own scoring code, run through the CRM's sales functions (bookings, outcomes, acceptances with payment plans, payments through the fake Bold route).
