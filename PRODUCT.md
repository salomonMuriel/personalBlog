# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Owners and general managers of traditional Colombian PYMEs: under USD 2M a year, roughly 5 to 40 people. Clothing
brands, restaurants, distributors, importers, service firms, a bridal shop. The business already sells and works, but
the operation runs on WhatsApp, a pile of Excel files that don't match, paper and people copying data from one place to
another. They feel slow, stuck in the stone age, and dependent on the one person who knows how each file works. Most
are not technical and arrive from LinkedIn, a talk or a referral, usually on a phone.

Secondary audiences with their own pages: people starting something (mentoring) and event organisers (talks).

## Product Purpose

Salomón Muriel's personal consulting practice. He builds a company its own operating system ("sistema operativo"), the
custom software that replaces the spreadsheets, paper and copy-paste, built around how the company already works. He
connects it to the tools they keep. Success on the site is a WhatsApp message, a completed qualification questionnaire
or a booked exploration call.

## Positioning

One person does the strategy and writes the code, so nothing is lost between a consultant's deck and a vendor's
interpretation. He builds fast with AI agents instead of subcontracting. The code, accounts and access belong to the
client, there is no monthly per-seat fee, and the team is taught to change the system themselves. It is built one
process at a time, starting where it hurts most.

## Operating Context

The visitor compares this against buying an off-the-shelf program, hiring a software agency, or carrying on with Excel.
The first conversation happens on WhatsApp (+57 313 246 5100) or a Cal.com call. The engagement runs about 16 weeks:
understanding the operation, a diagnosis with a first working piece, then building.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Spanish first, natural Colombian Spanish, always tú. English is a translation.
- No prices in any form, and no comment about not showing prices.
- No blog.
- No invented testimonials, clients, figures or quotes.
- «Chinomático» is never explained or attributed. The hero's dictionary entry teaches it.
- Ignia never appears in the home hero, and is never given as a reason for limited capacity.
- Mentoring never competes with Ignia's Action Lab.
- The site is Astro 6 + Tailwind 4 + Keystatic on Vercel. Content pages are prerendered, and Lighthouse mobile must
  stay 95+ in all four categories.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Salomón Muriel. The offer's working name with clients: «sistema operativo» (Aura Novias and Visible bought
  exactly this).
- Voice: plain, direct, a little dry. Short sentences, no hype, no exclamation marks.
- The «chinomático» idea and the «sistema de cajitas» (a company is a set of boxes that should be connected) are his.

## Product Principles

- Talk about the visitor's operation in their own words before talking about software.
- Show real systems he built, never mockups passed off as clients.
- Every screen offers one clear next step: the questionnaire or WhatsApp.
- Be honest about fit: say who this is not for and point them elsewhere.
